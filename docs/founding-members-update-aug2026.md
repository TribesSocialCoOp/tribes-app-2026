## 🎓 Cornell Wants to Study How We Govern

We were interviewed today by students out of Cornell University's [TrAuSt project](https://traust.infosci.cornell.edu/), who are researching and building tools around trust and civic discourse online. They were genuinely interested in our governance process and the kinds of discussions it's fostered here.

Great first conversation. We share a goal with the TrAuSt team: promoting good-faith, civic discourse online. As a FOSS platform, there may be room to work together going forward. Thanks for the time today, TrAuSt!

---

## 🌐 Next Up: Federation

Building a community from scratch is real work, and that's a big part of why people default to building where the audience already is. Federation is how we chip away at that.

The plan is to tap into the fediverse intentionally: curate good content from it (and contribute back), while we keep building the co-op model and growing our own base. Done right, this lets us syndicate our Tribes founders out to the wider fediverse, and pull good content in from other platforms too.

I'm hoping to get the governance proposal up this week. More to come when I release it for discussion and voting soon.
